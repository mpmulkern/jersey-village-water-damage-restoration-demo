Mold Remediation Cost in Houston: 2026 Guide

Quick Answer

Most residential mold remediation jobs in the Jersey Village / Houston area cost $1,100–$6,500, depending on affected square footage, whether HVAC ductwork is involved, and how much porous material (drywall, carpet padding, insulation) has to be removed rather than just cleaned. Get a free on-site assessment before assuming either the low or high end applies to your situation.

What Drives Mold Remediation Cost Up or Down

Houston's humidity means mold remediation pricing varies more by scope than by location. The main factors we weigh on every estimate:

  • Affected square footage — a single bathroom patch costs far less than a whole-room or multi-room job
  • Whether mold has spread into HVAC ductwork, which needs specialized duct cleaning on top of surface remediation
  • Surface type — porous materials like drywall, carpet padding, and insulation usually must be removed and replaced, not just cleaned
  • How long the mold has been growing before treatment starts — early treatment is cheaper than remediation after months of unseen growth
  • Whether containment and HEPA air scrubbing are needed to stop spores spreading during the work

Typical Price Ranges

Small, isolated patch (under 10 sq ft)

$500–$1,200 — surface cleaning and minor containment, typically one room, no structural removal.

Single room, moderate spread

$1,100–$3,500 — containment, HEPA air scrubbing, some drywall or flooring removal and replacement.

Multi-room or HVAC-involved

$3,500–$6,500 — duct cleaning, multiple containment zones, larger material removal and replacement.

Whole-house after major flooding

$6,500–$10,000+ — extensive structural removal, multi-day containment and drying, full clearance testing.

Does Insurance Cover It?

Insurance typically covers mold remediation when it results directly from a covered water event, such as a burst pipe, subject to your policy's mold sub-limits (often capped between $1,000 and $10,000). Mold from long-term humidity or a maintenance issue is usually excluded. We document the water source and mold extent from the first visit to support your claim either way — see our FAQ page for more on how we work with adjusters.

Is a Cheap Quote a Red Flag?

Often, yes. A quote well below market rate for the described square footage may mean surface cleaning only, without containment, air scrubbing, or post-remediation clearance testing — which lets the mold return within weeks in Houston's humidity. Ask whether the quote includes containment barriers, HEPA air scrubbing, and a clearance test before comparing price alone.
