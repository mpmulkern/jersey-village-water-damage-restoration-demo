Quick Answer: What should I do in the first hour after water damage?

Shut off the water source if it's safe to do so, move valuables away from standing water, and avoid using electrical outlets in wet areas. Then call a restoration company immediately — water damage compounds by the hour, and drying started within 24–48 hours dramatically lowers the risk of mold.
