Frequently Asked Questions

Is a sewage backup dangerous to clean up myself?

Yes. Sewage backups are classified as black water and carry serious bacterial and pathogen risk. We strongly recommend against DIY cleanup — our technicians use full PPE, hospital-grade disinfectants, and proper disposal procedures to make the area safe again.

What causes sewage backups in Jersey Village homes?

Common causes include tree root intrusion into sewer lines, municipal system overload during heavy storms, and clogged or aging plumbing. We identify the source as part of the cleanup so you can address the root cause.

Does insurance cover sewage cleanup?

Many homeowners policies cover sewage backup cleanup if you carry a sewer backup endorsement or rider; standard policies without that rider often exclude it. We document the incident thoroughly regardless, so you have what you need to file.
