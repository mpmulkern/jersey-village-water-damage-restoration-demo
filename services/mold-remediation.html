Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs of hidden mold in my home?

A persistent musty smell, discolored patches on walls or ceilings, peeling paint, or unexplained allergy symptoms that improve when you leave the house are common signs of hidden mold. Houston's humidity makes hidden mold behind walls and under flooring especially common after any water intrusion.

Does insurance cover mold remediation?

Mold remediation is often covered when the mold resulted directly from a covered water damage event, such as a burst pipe, subject to your policy's mold sub-limits. Mold from long-term neglect or humidity alone is typically not covered. We document the water source and mold extent to support your claim.

How long does mold remediation take?

Most residential mold remediation projects in the Jersey Village / Houston area take 2 to 5 days, depending on the affected area and whether containment and negative air pressure are required.
