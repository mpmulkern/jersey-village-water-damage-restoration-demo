Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help with flood damage after a hurricane or tropical storm?

Yes. We provide priority storm response for Jersey Village and Houston-metro homes affected by hurricane or tropical storm flooding, including water extraction, drying, and full insurance documentation for federal and private flood claims.

Is flood damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Standard homeowners insurance typically does not cover flood damage from rising water; that requires separate flood insurance (often NFIP). Wind-driven rain intrusion through a damaged roof or window may be covered under a standard policy. We document the damage source clearly to support whichever claim applies.

How quickly should storm damage cleanup start?

As soon as it's safe to enter the property. Standing water and wet materials left more than 24–48 hours sharply increase the risk of mold growth, especially in Houston's humidity. We prioritize storm-affected homes for same-day response when conditions allow.
