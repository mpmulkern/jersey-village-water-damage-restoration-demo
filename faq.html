Common Questions

What should I do in the first 24 hours after water damage?

Shut off the water source if safe, move valuables and electronics away from the water, avoid using outlets in wet areas, and call a restoration company immediately. Do not wait to see if it dries on its own — standing water and wet materials left more than 24–48 hours sharply increase the risk of mold, especially in Houston's humidity. Photograph the damage before cleanup begins for your insurance claim.

Does insurance cover mold remediation?

Mold remediation is often covered when it resulted directly from a covered water damage event, such as a burst pipe, subject to your policy's mold sub-limits. Mold caused by long-term neglect or ambient humidity alone is typically not covered. We document the water source and mold extent from the first visit to support whichever claim applies.

What are the signs of hidden mold?

A persistent musty smell, discolored patches on walls or ceilings, peeling or bubbling paint, and allergy-like symptoms that improve when you leave the house are common signs of hidden mold. Any home with a past water intrusion in Houston's climate should be inspected even without visible mold.

How fast can you respond to a water emergency in Jersey Village?

We dispatch 24/7 and are typically on-site within an hour for active water emergencies in Jersey Village and the surrounding Houston metro area.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

Most residential water damage restoration jobs in the Jersey Village / Houston area run $1,500–$7,500 depending on the extent of water intrusion and affected materials. We provide a free on-site assessment and written estimate before any work begins.

Do you work directly with my insurance company?

Yes. We document damage thoroughly with photos and moisture readings from the first visit and can meet with your insurance adjuster on-site to support your claim.

Is it safe to stay in my home during water damage restoration?

In most cases, yes, unless the damage involves sewage contamination, structural risk, or extensive mold, in which case we'll advise you clearly. Air movers and dehumidifiers can be noisy but are safe to be around.

How long does the drying process take?

Structural drying typically takes 3 to 5 days with daily moisture monitoring, though it can extend longer for extensive water intrusion or dense materials like hardwood flooring.

Do you handle sewage backups too?

Yes. Sewage backups require specialized black-water cleanup with full PPE and hospital-grade decontamination, which we provide 24/7 alongside our standard water damage services.
